2. Email Popup for New Customers (Yes)
Purpose

Capture emails before people bounce.

Best Popup Offer

“Get the Weekly Drop Digest (Top 10 domains + trends)”

Not:

“Subscribe for updates”

Make it specific.

Popup Copy (High-Converting)
Headline

Get the Best Domains Dropping This Week

Subtext

Weekly digest of the top scored drops + renewal trap warnings.

Input

[Email address]

Button:
Send Me the Digest

Small:
No spam. Unsubscribe anytime.

Timing Rules (Do it right)

Show after 15–30 seconds

Or on exit intent

Never immediately

3. Newsletter + Product Flywheel

This creates the perfect funnel:

Visitor → Email digest → Signup → Watchlist → Pro upgrade

Funnel Steps

User joins newsletter (no account needed)

Receives weekly list of top drops

Clicks into the app

Creates free account to watch domains

Hits Pro triggers (alerts, saved searches)

Converts

This is the cleanest acquisition loop.
