Here’s the best onboarding process for **TLDTerminal Lite** — designed for fast activation, habit formation, and natural Pro upgrades.

This is the exact MVP onboarding flow you should ship.

---

# Onboarding Goals (MVP)

You want every new user to reach this moment within 2 minutes:

## “I just found a domain worth watching.”

Onboarding should create:

* immediate value (feed)
* first action (watch)
* upgrade trigger (alerts/saved searches)

---

# User Onboarding Flow (Step-by-Step)

---

## 1. Visitor Landing (Logged Out)

### Experience

User lands on homepage and immediately sees:

* sample Daily Drop Feed preview
* “Premium renewal warning” example
* clear CTA

### CTA

**Start Free Account**

No pricing wall yet.

---

## 2. Signup (Minimal Friction)

### Required fields only:

* Email
* Password

Optional later:

* Name
* Investor preferences

### Copy

“Create your free account to watch domains and track drops.”

---

## 3. First Login → Welcome Screen (1-time)

### Title

**Welcome to TLDTerminal**

### Message

“Your daily ranked drop feed is live. Let’s personalize it.”

Button:
**Set Preferences (30 seconds)**

Skip option:
“Skip for now”

---

## 4. Preference Setup (Ultra Light)

Only ask 3 things:

### Step 1: Preferred Extensions

Checkbox pills:

* .com
* .io
* .ai
* Other

### Step 2: Investor Style

Choose one:

* Brandable names
* Keyword domains
* Short domains
* AI/startup niches

### Step 3: Renewal Sensitivity

Dropdown:

* Under $30/year (recommended)
* Under $100/year
* Show all

Save button:
**Generate My Feed**

---

## 5. Activation Moment: First Feed View

User lands directly in:

## Daily Drop Feed (personalized)

Show:

* Top 25 domains
* Score badges
* Drop countdown
* Premium renewal warnings

Add a helper tooltip:

👉 “Click Watch to track domains and get alerts before they drop.”

---

## 6. First Key Action: Watch a Domain

When user clicks Watch:

Toast:
✅ Added to Watchlist

Immediately show:

“Watched domains can send drop alerts on Pro.”

Subtle upgrade hook.

---

## 7. Watchlist Page Prompt

After 2–3 watched domains:

Banner:

**Want alerts before these domains drop?**
Pro investors get notified automatically.

Button:
**Start Pro Trial**

---

## 8. Saved Search Hook (Best Upgrade Trigger)

When user clicks “New Saved Search”:

Locked modal:

**Automate your deal flow with Pro**
Saved searches alert you when high-score domains appear.

CTA:
**Start 7-Day Trial**

---

## 9. AI Builder Teaser (Secondary Hook)

Sidebar item:

AI Builder (Pro)

Click → upsell screen:

“Generate investable domain ideas instantly.”

CTA:
Unlock Pro

---

## 10. Pro Trial Start (Moment of Delight)

After upgrade:

Success screen:

**Welcome to Pro**
Alerts and saved searches are now active.

Next action button:

**Create Your First Saved Search**

Optional confetti here only.

---

# Starter vs Pro Onboarding Experience

## Starter user completes:

* Set preferences
* Watch 3 domains
* Hit upgrade trigger naturally

## Pro user completes:

* Create saved search
* Enable alerts
* Receive first notification

That’s retention.
