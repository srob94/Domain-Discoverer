1. Homepage / Marketing Site Navigation
Purpose

Convert visitors → signup → upgrade.

Keep it minimal, trust-building, not feature-heavy.

Top Nav (Logged Out)

Product

Pricing

FAQ (optional)

Login

Get Started Free (primary CTA)

That’s it.

Why so few?

Visitors don’t want dashboards yet — they want:

what is this?

why trust it?

how much?

how do I start?

Homepage Sections (Scroll-Based)

Instead of extra pages:

Hero

Pro features

Testimonials (later)

CTA

6. Key Rule
Marketing nav answers:

“What is this and why should I trust it?”

Portal nav answers:

“What should I buy today and how do I track it?”

Do not mix them.
